## 🧠 About Me

I'm a robotics and automation engineer in the making — a Mechatronics & Control Engineering student at **UET Lahore** 🇵🇰 — focused on building autonomous systems that sense, decide, and move reliably in the real world. That means owning the full stack underneath a robot: mechanical design, control, and the software that ties them together, because a robot doesn't care which discipline is "responsible" when something doesn't sense right or doesn't move right.

I go deep before I go wide. Before I trust a system, I want to know the math behind it, the electrons moving through it, and the code steering it. That habit has taken me from reverse-engineering a development board down to its clock network, to designing the structural mast of a robot and proving on paper that it won't bend more than 5 mm before I ever cut metal.

I've shipped work across three different worlds — freelance client contracts, university research labs, and personal hardware builds — and I hold all three to the same bar: it has to actually work, not just simulate well.

**Currently:** Hardware lead on an autonomous UV-C disinfection robot, and running an active freelance CAD/PCB practice on the side.
**Long-term:** Building toward a product or startup of my own, and becoming someone who can take a robotic system from a blank sheet to a working, deployed machine — independently.
